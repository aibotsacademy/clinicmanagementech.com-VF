FAQ

What clinics ask us.

Do I have to replace my current system?

No. We start where your clinic needs it most and connect the rest step by step.

Does the facial analysis replace a medical assessment?

No. It supports the doctor; the decision is always theirs.

Which brands can I sell?

The ones your clinic already carries, plus your own line.

Where do the before & after photos come from?

From your own patients, captured with the app and shared only with their consent.

Can you guarantee ChatGPT will recommend my clinic?

No one can. We improve the signals AI relies on and measure the result every month.

Can I use my own brand?

Yes. The app and the shop carry your clinic's identity.
